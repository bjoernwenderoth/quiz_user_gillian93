# PROJECT X

MODE: Operate. A guided private-access challenge, followed by a cinematic gift reveal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ten correct keys unlock one surprise. Keep the occasion and prize out of every visible screen and metadata until the explicit unlock. No score or penalty for retries.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the existing olive anthracite, warm ivory and champagne system. Keep local Manrope and DM Sans, outlined controls, sage and peach feedback, and short Motion transitions. Replace birthday decoration with a large typographic X, repeated quietly on symbolic tickets.

STORY: Simulated login, verification, briefing, ten illustrated questions, halfway pause, completion, deliberate unlock, decryption, birthday reveal, two keepsake tickets and a PNG export.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On desktop an oversized X occupies the left half, the PROJECT X heading and access form occupy the right. On phones the X becomes a shallow banner above the form. Questions pair locally hosted, consistently graded images with four large answer controls. The main action stays explicit.

FORM: The user's specified nine-state flow; code-first extension of the established system. No new concept roll or replacement palette.

FINISH: Browser checks on desktop and mobile, validated PNG downloads, independent finish review, maintained design-system reference and current feature documentation. No deployment or push.

## Constraints and evidence

No credentials stored or transmitted. Static client state is not real authentication. Audio stays unconfigured until an official video ID is supplied; no external player before user interaction. Images are replaceable atmospheric stock motifs, not artist portraits or game screenshots. Reduced motion, keyboard focus, retry feedback and session recovery are tested. The screenshot evidence is in `.impeccable/review/`; current setup and content instructions belong to README.md.
