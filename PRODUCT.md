## Platform
web

## Stack
React, strict TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind v4, Motion, Lucide, local Manrope and DM Sans fonts. html-to-image for local PNG export.

## Product Purpose
A private-access story for Gillian. Ten illustrated questions unlock a surprise gift: two symbolic concert tickets for Samra & Capital Bra, Berlin lebt 3, 01.04.2027, Sporthalle Hamburg. The birthday occasion and gift stay hidden in the visible interface until the explicit unlock.

## Users
Gillian, opening a QR-code link on a phone. This is a personal one-session experience, with desktop support.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static frontend with simulated login, no real authentication. Validate email syntax and nonempty password but never store or transmit credentials. Retry wrong answers. Pause after question five. Completion precedes explicit unlock, decryption and reveal. Session storage preserves progress when available. Two symbolic keepsake tickets, one downloadable PNG. Official YouTube trailer can be configured later and starts only after user interaction. No original local music. GitHub Pages under quiz_user_gillian93. No publication or push requested.

## Brand Commitments
German language, dark premium experience, existing olive anthracite and champagne palette, local typography, restrained cinematic transitions. Strong focus states, phone usability and reduced-motion support.

## Evidence on Hand
The user's exact ten-question catalog, named recipient, gift details and personal message direction. Local stock motifs with recorded origins. No official trailer source supplied. Event details are user-supplied, not independently verified.

## Product Principles
- One question at a time, no time pressure or penalty for retrying.
- Reveal the occasion only after the final unlock action.
- Keep content, flow and presentation separate.
- Make saved progress recoverable and image export understandable on phones.
